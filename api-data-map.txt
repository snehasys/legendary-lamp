## Available Data from Yahoo Finance via Forge API

### get_stock_chart (meta)
- regularMarketPrice, regularMarketDayHigh, regularMarketDayLow
- regularMarketVolume, regularMarketTime
- fiftyTwoWeekHigh, fiftyTwoWeekLow
- chartPreviousClose
- longName, shortName, exchangeName, currency
- Price history (timestamps, open, high, low, close, volume)

### get_stock_insights
- instrumentInfo.technicalEvents (short/mid/long term outlook with direction + score)
- instrumentInfo.keyTechnicals (support, resistance, stopLoss)
- instrumentInfo.valuation (relativeValue, description, discount, provider)
- companySnapshot.company (SCORING data only: innovativeness, hiring, sustainability, insiderSentiments, earningsReports, dividends)
- companySnapshot.sector (same scoring keys for sector comparison)
- companySnapshot.sectorInfo (sector name)
- recommendation (may be empty - {})
- events (technical events like CCI)
- reports (100 analyst reports from Argus Research etc.)
- sigDevs (significant developments with headline + date)

### get_stock_profile
- summaryProfile only: longBusinessSummary, sector, industry, fullTimeEmployees, website, phone, address, city, state, country, companyOfficers

### get_stock_holders
- insiderHolders.holders (name, relation, transactionDescription, latestTransDate, positionDirect)
- NO netSharePurchaseActivity
- NO institutionOwnership

### get_stock_sec_filing
- secFilings.filings (date, type, title, edgarUrl, exhibits[])
- Types include: SD, 8-K, 10-Q, 10-K, etc.

## What We CANNOT Get:
- PE ratio, Forward PE, PEG, PB, PS
- EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA
- Market Cap, Enterprise Value, Shares Outstanding
- Gross Margin, Operating Margin, Net Margin
- ROE, ROA, Debt/Equity, Current Ratio
- Revenue Growth, Earnings Growth
- Dividend Yield
- Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow data
- Institutional ownership percentages

## Conclusion:
The Ratios tab and Financial Statement tabs will show empty states.
The app's strength is in: Price data, Technical Analysis, Insights, Insider Holdings, SEC Filings, Company Profile.
